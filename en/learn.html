FAQ

What everyone asks

Is it really free?

Yes. All the content on this site is free and you do not need to sign up. Your progress is saved in your own browser.

Is the course in English?

Not yet. The lessons are currently in Spanish; an English version is planned. Your browser's translation feature works reasonably well in the meantime.

Do I need money to start?

No. The first phases are theory and practice on a simulation account. Trading real money is not recommended until phase 9, and always starting with micros (MES or MNQ).

How long will it take?

About 13 weeks of learning at 5–7 hours a week, plus several months of practice in simulation. What matters is completing each checklist, not the date.

Which platform do I need?

Any platform that shows the DOM and footprint: NinjaTrader 8, Sierra Chart, ATAS or Quantower, among others. Phase 5 explains how to set it up in simulation.

Why NQ and ES?

They are the most traded US index futures, with deep liquidity and micro versions to start with little risk.
