OPB DOM Order flow AddOn

A price ladder that brings together, in a single window, the order book, what trades at each level and how liquidity changes, so you can read who is pushing and who is defending before you enter.

  • Level 2 book, market volume (@Bid / @Ask), delta per level and session volume profile with POC, VAH and VAL.
  • P/S column (pull / stack): liquidity added or removed without trading, checked against real book recordings of NQ, MNQ and ES.
  • Absorption and pull-on-touch detector, marked on the price, with sensitivity that adapts to each instrument.
  • Alerts for walls, aggression, pulls and stacks near price, as text or sound.
  • Key RTH and ETH levels, trading from the DOM with ATM strategies and profiles saved per instrument.

OPB Limitador Risk management AddOn

Designed for evaluation and funded accounts: set your limits before you start and, if you hit them, the account stays locked until the session closes, even if you restart NinjaTrader.

  • Maximum daily loss per account and per instrument (NQ, MNQ or global), with a safety buffer.
  • Drawdown protection and maximum contracts in mini equivalents, with a scaling ladder based on accumulated profit.
  • Profiles per prop firm and mandatory mode: without activating the limiter that day, no positions can be opened.

This is what it looks like when the daily limit is hit: the account is locked and the panel shows how long until it unlocks and how much drawdown margin is left.
