Getting started

Requirements

  • NinjaTrader 8 installed on Windows.
  • Market data with book depth (Level 2) for the order flow tools.
  • Designed and tested on NQ, MNQ and ES futures.

Installing an AddOn in NinjaTrader 8

This is NinjaTrader's general procedure for importing add-ons. Tool-specific details will be in each manual.

  1. Download the tool's .zip file. Do not unzip it.
  2. In the NinjaTrader Control Center, open Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On…
  3. Select the .zip file and confirm the import.
  4. If NinjaTrader asks, restart it so the tool appears in its menus.

FAQ

Do they work on platforms other than NinjaTrader 8?

No. They are AddOns and indicators written for NinjaTrader 8.

Do they give buy or sell signals?

No. They are tools for visualisation, execution and risk control. Trading decisions are always yours.

When will they be available?

They are not for sale yet. Write to contacto@opb-labs.com and I will let you know when they are.

Where can I see them working?

In the sessions on the YouTube channel @opb_labs (videos in Spanish).
