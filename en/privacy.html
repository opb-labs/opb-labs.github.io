What data I process and why

Emails you send me. If you write to an @opb-labs.com address, I process your email address, your name if you give it and the content of the message in order to answer your query. The legal basis is your consent when writing to me and the legitimate interest in answering your query (Article 6(1)(a) and (f) of the General Data Protection Regulation). I keep messages for as long as the conversation lasts and afterwards only for the time needed to handle any possible claims. I do not use your data to send you advertising or share it with third parties.

Technical browsing data. To display the website and protect it against attacks, the hosting provider technically processes data such as your IP address and browser type. The owner does not use this data to identify you or build profiles.

Providers involved

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: website hosting, domain management and forwarding of @opb-labs.com email.
  • Google LLC (Gmail): the mailbox where emails are received and answered.

Both providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not set analytics, advertising or social media cookies, and fonts are served from the site's own domain, with no connections to third parties.

The course saves in your browser's local storage (localStorage) which exercises you have ticked, so you can see your progress when you come back. That data only exists on your device, is not sent to any server and you can delete it at any time from your browser settings. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary technical cookies for the site's security, which do not require consent.

Links to YouTube and other sites

Videos are not embedded: when you click a link to YouTube or another website you leave this site and that service's privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to contacto@opb-labs.com. If you believe they have not been properly addressed, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Changes to this policy

If the site adds forms, product sales or other services in the future, this policy will be updated before they go live.
